R3 · decided
Drag-and-drop, and it must be obviously drag-and-drop.
Acceptance rule for PartTile: a first-time player must see that the tile moves before touching anything. Five signals, all required:
1. Grab handle (⋮⋮) on every tile, cursor turns to a hand on hover.
2. The hint text on the tile says the verb: "drag ↑" (mobile: "tap to add").
3. The empty slot says the verb too: "drag a step here…".
4. On the first build screen of a session, the top tile does one short wiggle and the first slot pulses amber once (skipped when reduced-motion is on).
5. Tap still works as a fallback and animates the tile sliding into the slot, so nobody is stuck.

You just learned the term Guardrails

What it means

Guardrails are the limits you set on what your bot may do or say, so a clever customer cannot talk it into anything.

Why you care

Without them your bot will promise discounts, delivery times and opinions you never approved, and you pay for every one.

infoIn the real world

Picking safe options like this is only the start. A real bot needs much stronger guardrails, enforced in code and tested against attackers.
