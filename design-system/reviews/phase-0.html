2 · Open the app

In a terminal, inside the pizza-bot folder (branch docs/interview-1-feedback):

npm run dev

Then open the address it prints (usually http://localhost:5173) on your phone-sized browser window, or your phone on the same Wi-Fi. Phone width matters: that is what Klára saw.

Answer each with a yes or no. Trust the first impression; do not go back and forth.
