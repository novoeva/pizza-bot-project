Linear · PIZZA-31 · bug · Token game · from interview #3 (Nina, 2026-09-04)

The Token game predicts the next token, not the next word

What Nina hit: "pizza" is at least two tokens, so a model never predicts a whole word. But the "next token" part of the game let you guess whole words. Eva confirmed it: factually wrong for what the game teaches.

What changed (3 things)

  1. The bot now writes "pepperoni" piece by piece, with the exact pieces screen 1 chopped it into (pep · per · on · i). Each round stops mid-word: the pieces already written are on screen as chips, then an empty slot. You call the next piece.
  2. The options are pieces, not words. Round 1: per · si · tide · py. Round 2: on · mint · s · ed. Round 3, once the word is done, shows that a whole short word or a comma can be one token too: pizza · and · , · s.
  3. After the pick the pieces snap together in a new "Snapped together" box: pep + per = pepper… "2 more picks (on, i) and the word is done." The ranking now also says what each piece would have made (Pepsi, peptide, peppy).
