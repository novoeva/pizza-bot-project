The MCP game now makes you do the old way before it shows you the new one

Eva, Phase 4 review: "I'm not sure what this is supposed to teach me. There is no simulation."
Nina, interview #3: the "universal port" sentence made no sense ("port" does not fit this world; everyone understands "integration"). "Instead of wiring each one by hand" made her try to do it by hand first, and there was nothing to do. The hard step had no "aha", because the painful version did not simulate any pain. The easy step (click, click, green) worked great.

What changed (3 things)

  1. The old way is now work you do, not text you read. Wiring Menu is a five-step wizard: pick the data format, match three field names, copy the access key (two look-alikes next to it), run a test, fix what broke. Wrong taps stay wrong until you fix them. Then Delivery, all five steps again. Bookings is one tap that stands for all five. Then the Loyalty app arrives: "start over from step 1?" Weeks and taps pile up on screen. The MCP step stays click, click, green.
  2. "Universal port" is gone. The game and the term card now say "one shared standard" and "one integration instead of one per system". MCP is explained as a standard (Model Context Protocol), never as a port.
  3. A closing picture. After the stranger's stock tracker connects, and again on the recap: left, five custom wires criss-crossing into your bot; right, the same five systems plugged into one MCP hub. With your real numbers under each side (weeks, taps).

Five yes/no questions

Q1 · part 1, wiring Menu
Did the five steps feel like tedious work, and did you find yourself wanting it to stop?
Tap through: format (XML), the three field names, the access key (mind the look-alikes), Run the test, the fix. Wrong taps show a red one-liner and you stay on the step.
Expected yes. That is the point: the pain is felt, not read.
Q2 · part 1, after Delivery
When Bookings became one tap for "all five steps again", was that a relief, or did you want to skip it sooner?
You do Menu and Delivery in full, Bookings is the one-tap version, then Loyalty asks "start over from step 1?"
Expected relief at the right moment (twice in full is enough). If you wanted out after Menu alone, say so.
Q3 · part 2, the MCP step
Without reading the definition on the left, can you say in one sentence what MCP solves?
Tap "Teach your bot the standard (MCP)", then Connect on each of the four systems. The two counters compare your weeks and taps with 0 wks / 5 taps.
Expected yes, something like "one standard so I don't build an integration per system".
Q4 · the words
"One shared standard" / "speaks MCP" / "custom integration": do these words make sense, and is "port" gone everywhere you looked?
Check the part 2 instruction, the blue bot card badge, the recap cards, and the Workshop card for MCP.
Expected yes.
Q5 · end of part 3, the picture
Does the wires-vs-hub diagram say "many custom wires vs one standard" on its own, without the captions?
After you Connect the stock tracker, the diagram appears under the green callout. Left: five amber wires crossing into your bot. Right: five straight green lines into an MCP hub.
Expected yes, and it fits on a phone in two side-by-side panels without horizontal scroll.
