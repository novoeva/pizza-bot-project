Build the bot as a flow, not a robot

The robot shows that you collected parts. A flow shows what the parts do. Lada described it without prompting: an input, something that processes it, the AI model, something that writes an order somewhere. Nina tried to drag the term cards as if they were flow components. The board below is that picture.

The board

Click any node or chip to mark it learned. What a new player sees at 0 / 12 is only shadows: the shape of a bot, twelve numbered slots, and a clear place to start.

Two kinds of terms

Nina's split (token is LLM 101, RAG is LLM 301) and Eva's (basics vs advanced) land on the same line. Four terms are properties of the model. Eight terms are things you wire around it. The picture makes the split visible without adding tiers or locks.

What it answers from the interviews

  • Lada, learning from the end. At 12 / 12 the board is a working diagram and a test order runs through it. He gets to see the finished bot without the games changing.
  • Lada, wait, what is progress? Progress is wires turning solid. Nobody has to be told the robot is a progress bar.
  • Nina, number them and say where to start. The flow reads left to right and the numbers follow it. Twelve shadows are visible at once, so the promise of 12 is on screen from the first second.
  • Nina, components is confusing. On a flow the word becomes true: eight terms are literally boxes. The other four are called what they are, settings.
  • Nina wanted to drag the cards. A canvas is what she expected to see. Clicking a shadow opens its game.
  • Nina, the MCP picture. Her spokes-into-one-center drawing is built into the standing board, not only into one recap screen.
  • Nina, too many choices. Two groups of terms instead of a flat list of twelve. The recommended path is still free, not locked.

What it costs

Phone fit

The robot fit on one screen. The flow has fifteen boxes and scrolls on a phone. The model node with its four chips is the main saving. Hiding the grey scenery boxes until their neighbour is learned would save more.

Robot art elsewhere

The landing page steps and the recap line Snapped onto your bot · Voice box both assume a robot. They would say wired into your flow instead. Nina did not understand the voice box anyway.

Hallucination is a stretch

It is a failure, not a part. Here it is a warning light inside the model. The honest alternative is to show it as the bad reply that appears when tool use is missing, which is more work.

The games do not change

Same twelve terms, same definition → simulation → recap framework. The new board is a drop-in for the robot component on the Workshop screen. It takes the same list of completed terms.
