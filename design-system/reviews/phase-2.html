Pizza Bot · Phase 2 · choices and building

How to review Phase 2 in 15 minutes

Phase 1 approved. Phase 2 is the part Klára stumbled on: choices that did not look tappable. Two new things. Answers now use one card with a radio circle or a chevron. Building the bot now uses amber part tiles you drag (or tap) into slots.

1 · What changed

  1. Every answer is a SelectableCard. A circle on the left for "pick one" (Guardrails limits, Prompt round 3), a chevron on the right for "tap one and move on" (Prompt rounds 1–2, Tool use, Token, Temperature). Each group has a label and says "Pick one" or "Tap one".
  2. Building the bot is drag-and-drop. Skill (steps → playbook), Agent (actions → to-do list), RAG (pages → handed to the bot), Memory (facts → hard drive). Amber tiles with a grab handle; dashed slots that say "drag a step here…"; the next slot lights up "Drop here" while you drag; ✕ takes a part back.
  3. Tap still works on every tile, so a phone user is never stuck. On touch screens the tile says "tap to add" instead of "drag ↑".
  4. First time only: the first tile wiggles and the first slot pulses for two seconds, once per session.
  5. Hallucination keeps its two big Trust / Made up buttons. MCP keeps its row buttons. Both unchanged.
