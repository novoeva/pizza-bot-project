Interview #1 (Klára) · Linear PIZZA-14 + PIZZA-15 · Temperature game · round 1

The favourite is no longer "the right answer", and rolling now proves something

What Klára hit (PIZZA-14): the favourite ("classic") stayed highlighted wherever she dragged the slider, so the point of a high temperature never showed. The maths was right all along, classic goes from ~87 % at the bottom to ~28 % at the top, but the picture said "classic is correct" the whole time.
What Klára hit (PIZZA-15): "Roll again" only changed the sentence. She could not see what it was for, and the rolled word never connected back to the list of probabilities.

What changed (3 things)

  1. No word is pinned as the favourite. All bars are the same cheese colour, the favourite is only ever the tallest bar, bar widths animate as you drag, and one line under the bars reads the lead straight off the maths: "classic is 7.1× more likely than the runner-up. It wins almost every roll." at 0.2, "classic is 1.2× … Barely ahead: almost any word can win." at 2.0.
  2. The rolled word lights up in the list, not just in the sentence. Blue chip + dice icon on the row the bot picked, the same blue as the word in the sentence. The sub-line under the sentence says at which dial position it was rolled. Move the dial and the row mark goes away (the odds on screen are no longer the odds it was drawn from).
  3. The experiment is guided: roll low, then roll high. The step instruction says so, the dial now starts low (0.4), the pinned bar always has the roll button ("Roll again at 0.4"), and a new "Your rolls so far" panel keeps a tally per setting: Low: classic ×4 · pepperoni ×1 / High: dill pickle ×2 · classic ×1 …. "Next" appears once you have rolled at both ends (or after six rolls anywhere).

Five yes/no questions

Q1 · game open, no roll yet, drag the dial slowly from 0.2 to 2.0
Can you see the favourite's lead collapse without reading any text?
The classic bar should shrink from nearly full to about a quarter while the small words grow, and no row should look "selected" or "correct" at any point. The line under the bars goes from "7.1×" to "1.2×" as a second signal.
Expected yes.
Q2 · dial at 0.4, tap Roll the dice once
Is it obvious which row in the list the bot picked, and that it is the same word as in the sentence?
One row gets a blue chip and a dice icon; the sentence shows the same word in the same blue; the sub-line reads "Rolled at 0.4 · Predictable".
Expected yes.
Q3 · after that first roll, without doing anything else
Do you know what you are supposed to do next?
The instruction says roll low then high; the tally panel's High row reads "No rolls yet, drag the dial up and roll"; the top-right of the step line shows "Low ✓ High ·"; the pinned bar only offers Roll again, no Next yet.
Expected yes, drag the dial up and roll.
Q4 · three rolls at 0.4, then three rolls at 2.0, look at "Your rolls so far"
Does the tally make the difference between low and high visible?
Low should be nearly all classic; High should be a mix, often with a long shot in it. It is random, so once in a while High also shows classic ×2, that is honest. Roll a couple more times if it does.
Expected yes.
Q5 · the gate
Is it fine that "Next: pick the right setting" only appears after you have rolled both low and high (or six times anywhere)?
Before, one roll unlocked Next. Now the experiment is required. The six-roll escape hatch means nobody can get stuck.
Expected yes. If no, I make Next available after the first roll again and keep only the nudges.
