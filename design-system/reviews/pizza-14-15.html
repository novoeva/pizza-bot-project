Interview #1 (Klára) · Linear PIZZA-14 + PIZZA-15 · Temperature game · round 2

Four tokens, one dial, and the pick really changes at the top

Round 1 feedback (Eva): "Your rolls so far" made no sense, delete it. The real problems: (1) the same word, "classic", is always on top no matter where the dial is; (2) the game says the bot picks tokens, but the options were words and phrases, so it was actively confusing. Use words that are one token each, about four of them.

What changed (3 things)

  1. Four options, each one token. classic · spicy · cheese · pickle. Short, common words that a real tokenizer keeps whole, so "the bot picks a token" and "the bot picks one of these" are the same sentence. The panel now says "Chance of each next token · each option = one token". The recap lines say token too.
  2. The top of the dial is nearly a coin flip. At 0.2 classic has 98 %. At 2.0 it is 31 · 26 · 23 · 20 %, so rolling up there lands on a different token most of the time, and the sentence visibly changes. (Below 1.0 the dial is the real temperature; above 1.0 it is stretched so four candidates can actually flatten.) Classic stays the biggest bar on every setting, that is honest: temperature changes the odds, never the order. What changes is what the roll lands on.
  3. Tally gone, lead line gone, gate gone. No "Your rolls so far" panel, no "classic is 1.2× more likely" sentence, and "Next" unlocks after the first roll like before. What is left: the sentence with the rolled token in blue, the dial, the four bars, the zone note, and the blue chip + dice on the row the bot picked.

Four yes/no questions

Q1 · dial at 0.4, tap Roll three times
Does the bot say "classic" every time, and is it clear why?
The classic bar is nearly full (85 %), the sentence reads "Tonight's special is the classic." on each roll, and the classic row carries the blue chip and the dice.
Expected yes.
Q2 · drag the dial to 2.0, tap Roll again four times
Does the pick change from roll to roll, so the point of "wild" lands without reading anything?
The four bars are almost level. Each roll the blue token in the sentence and the blue row jump around: spicy, classic, pickle… Once in a while two rolls in a row match; that is honest randomness, roll once more.
Expected yes.
Q3 · the list of four
Do "classic · spicy · cheese · pickle" read as tokens the bot picks between, with no word-vs-token confusion?
Each is one short word; the panel says each option = one token; the Token game (term 1) shows the same idea with pieces of "pepperoni".
Expected yes. If a different set of four would sit better (e.g. a wilder long shot than "pickle"), say which.
Q4 · classic is the tallest bar at every setting
Is it fine that classic keeps the biggest bar even at 2.0, as long as the roll lands elsewhere most of the time?
That is what temperature does: it shrinks the favourite's lead, it does not reorder the list. The alternative, shuffling the order at high temperature, would teach something false.
Expected yes. ❌ means: make the bars strictly equal at 2.0 (25 % each), which I can do as a pure teaching simplification.
