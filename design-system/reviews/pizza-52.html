The "Start here" / "Next up" sticker sits above the card, not on it

What Eva hit: on the Workshop the red "Start here" / "Next up" sticker overlaps the card it points at. It sits on the card's top-left corner and covers part of the number circle and crowds the title (seen on "2 · Temperature" with "Next up").

What changed (3 things)

  1. The sticker is in normal flow above the card instead of floating over it. Before, it was absolutely positioned with a reserved gap smaller than the sticker itself, so it hung down into the card.
  2. Only its bottom 8px straddle the card's top border. That is less than the card's padding at every width (16px on the phone, 10px in the laptop side column), so the number badge and the title are never covered.
  3. The gallery snippet uses the same markup (design-system/gallery.html, "Workshop term card"), so the reference stays in step with the component.
