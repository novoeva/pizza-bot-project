How to review Phase 4 in 10 minutes

Last phase of the plan. Step 1 of the framework: define the term before playing. Your call: show the full definition, not a teaser. Plus the four games that were still single-column now use the same two-column stage as the rest.

1 · What changed

  1. Definition first. The intro card on the left now reads: name, Definition, Your role. The old teaser line is gone. The same definition comes back on the end screen, so the arc is define → apply → repeat.
  2. Token, Context window, Memory and MCP use the two-column stage on every screen: intro on the left, "Game · …" card and the play on the right, progress strip on top. Before, they dropped the intro after the first screen and stacked everything in one column on desktop (FR-20).
  3. Every game now starts with a "Game · …" card telling you what to do on this screen, including Hallucination and the first screens of the four games above.
  4. No game logic changed.
